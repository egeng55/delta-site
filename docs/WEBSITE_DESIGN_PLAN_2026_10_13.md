# Website design plan - October 13, 2026

## Direction

Three related projects, three distinct jobs. References inform composition and
craft, not copied sequences. Retain the user's extended personal introduction.
More introduction means more personal substance, not simply a shorter journey.

### Eric Geng / Geng Solutions

The story is the person, the work, and what connects it. Preserve the Gothic E,
personal name, cinematic scale, project captures, and native scrolling. Replace
the lavender Gothic About/collage sequence with an editorial personal chapter:
an opening statement, Atlanta/Michigan context, and three connected directions
(software, digital experiences, ventures). Use only established biographical
facts. Do not invent dates, client outcomes, hobbies, endorsements, or portraits.

Keep an extended introduction and optional direct links to work. Each section
adds something: who Eric is, what he makes, how he approaches a build, what he
is exploring. Place Geng Solutions as the independent consulting practice,
Delta as his personal software, and Aegis as a separate venture concept.
Keep concept imagery and illustrative data labeled. Improve the contact journey
without implying the local draft composer sends mail.

### Delta

Preserve the nested triangle, dark blue/purple identity, and personal scenery as
decorative atmosphere. Move from a manifesto to a product narrative:

1. Delta: a clear introduction and direct route to examples or sign-in.
2. Three illustrative conversations, with inspectable context and uncertainty.
3. The continuity story: observe, remember, decide, explained concretely.
4. User control and account boundaries in plain language.
5. Current availability, development, and access.

Public examples make no network calls and show no real account data. They are
not live proof or a substitute for authenticated chat. Availability continues
to be governed by the existing public capability manifest. Scenery never
stands in for screenshots, evidence, or product outputs.

The product follow-through remains a separate functional increment: configure
the existing approved auth environment; verify account-separated chat; use an
existing server-owned conversation contract for saved threads if available.
Do not implement browser-local transcript storage as account memory or invent
an API. Do not modify auth/privacy policy as part of presentation work.

### Monte Management

Keep the current photography, monogram, editorial composition, and talent-first
presentation. Add early routes to viewing talent and booking while preserving
the image-led hero. Maintain honest email-draft and application behavior. Later
work can streamline enquiry delivery only with an approved destination.

## Shared craft, not shared templates

- Motion establishes identity, reveals relationships, or confirms interaction.
- Ordinary navigation and all content remain usable without motion.
- Atmospheric photographs are decorative; project media depicts actual work.
- Stable layouts, keyboard controls, visible focus, mobile-specific composition.
- No invented business results, artificial testimonials, or fake live activity.
- Preserve existing Next.js/static foundations and installed dependencies.

## Delivery sequence

1. Validate and locally preserve the portfolio's existing uncommitted baseline.
2. Implement the personal introduction, Delta examples/navigation, and Monte's
   early visitor paths. Keep repositories and commits separate.
3. Verify desktop/mobile, motion controls, example tabs/context disclosure,
   portfolio navigation, and existing test/build contracts.
4. Review locally before any deployment. Product auth and durable conversation
   work require explicit integration scope and verification, not visual claims.

No backend, CRM, provider, connector, access-policy, or deployment changes are
part of this presentation increment.
